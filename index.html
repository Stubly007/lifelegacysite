LifeLegacy

Keep a clear record of the gifts you make, so your family, executors and adviser know what was given and when. LifeLegacy shows where each gift is in the 7-year rule for UK inheritance tax, by tax year (6 April to 5 April).

Please note: this is an early demo with made-up sample data. It is a record-keeping tool, not tax advice. Anything you type in the demo stays in your own browser.
